# A detached dock and toasts don't slide in from under the bars

> **Issue:** [A detached dock and toasts don't slide in from under the bars](../issues.md#a-detached-dock-and-toasts-dont-slide-in-from-under-the-bars)
> **Status:** proposed

### Problem
On Hyprland (`supports.layerOrder`), every detached `EdgePopout` and bar popout slides in from the screen edge under the bars, its window on Top as `axiom-popout-under` ([Held off an edge › Sliding in from off the screen](../popouts/held-off-edge.md#sliding-in-from-off-the-screen)). The detached dock and the detached toasts (both `detached: true` by default) slide from their own window's edge instead, which is what every detached box does on sway and niri:
- **The dock** (`DockWindow.qml`): one always-mapped Top window, `axiom-dock`, ordered after the bars and border, its edge at the reserved line (`reservedHere`). The box slides `depth` out of it (`slide`), so it appears at that line. Held and reserving, its `exclusiveZone` ends at its box. A fullscreen window covers it (Top), so it never needs the Overlay layer.
- **The toasts** (`ToastStack.qml`, `area`): one always-mapped Overlay window, `axiom-notifications`, with `ExclusionMode.Normal` (inside the bars' and border's zones; `Ignore` over a fullscreen window). A toast slides from that window's edge on `slideEdge`, the screen edge nearest the stack (`ToastMotion.slideEdge`: bars play no part). With the default top-right stack and no bar on the right, that is the right edge: the screen edge, or the border's line.

With an opaque bar or border, a box cut at the window's edge and a box coming out from under the bar look about the same: in both, the first pixel shown is just past what reserves the edge. The difference shows with a translucent or blurred bar or border on the slide edge (an under-box shows through it), and in how far it travels. Whether the reserved line and `EdgePlacement`'s frame line ever differ, for example on a floating bar's gap, is not measured.

**Measured in a nested Hyprland** (2026-11-06; a scratch qs config with a Top `t-bar` at order 5 and a 40 px zone, and a Top `t-under` at order 6; `hyprctl layers` and grim):
1. `t-under` with an exclusive zone of 60 is arranged first: it sits at y 0 and **pushes the bar in** to y 60, and is drawn under it. So the dock's window can't take an under-the-bars order while it reserves; its zone would move the bars.
2. A live `set_layer` Top → Overlay → Top keeps the window mapped; on Overlay it draws over the bar, and back on Top it is arranged and drawn under the bar again.
3. Hyprland does **not** read `order` only as a surface maps: after the rule's order was changed to 3 at runtime (`hyprctl eval hl.layer_rule…`), the next arrange (a `set_layer`, or just a zone change) applied it, without a remap. The namespace is still fixed while mapped (Quickshell sends it only at map), so a window still can't change its order on its own, but the docs' "only as a surface maps" is wrong (`docs/dev/compositor/layer-arrangement.md`, `docs/dev/bar/layering-and-corners.md`, `qml/components/bar/AGENTS.md`, `ClosedLatch.qml`'s comment, `scripts/gen_docs.py`'s layers page).

### Options
#### A. Leave the slide as it is; correct the docs
The dock and toasts slide from their window's edge on every compositor, as every detached box already does on sway and niri. The entry is deleted, held-off-edge.md says so instead of pointing at an issue, and the "order only as a surface maps" wording is corrected to what was measured. No code; nothing to keep in step. Leaves the difference with a translucent bar or border on the slide edge.

#### B. Toasts under the bars, the dock as it is
With `supports.layerOrder`, the toasts' area window becomes a Top window with an under-the-bars namespace (a new `LayerStack` entry beside `axiom-popout-under`), `ExclusionMode.Ignore`, with its insets taken from the reserved zones rather than from its own exclusion; it moves to Overlay (live `set_layer`, measured above) over a fullscreen window and while the overlay is open, since a Top window is under the overlay. Toasts slide from the screen edge (`slideFrom` as for `EdgePopout`). Costs: a second layer rule, inset arithmetic that the window's exclusion now does for free, and a layer change while toasts are showing (a toast on screen as the overlay opens jumps from under the bars to over them; popouts avoid this by latching while open, which toasts can't). Rough guess, not measured: 80–150 lines of QML. It only changes how a toast looks when its slide edge has a translucent bar or border.

#### C. Both under the bars
B, plus the dock: because of measurement 1, a held reserving dock needs two windows: a zone-only `axiom-dock` window that keeps the zone, and the drawn box on its own window under the bars, reaching back to the screen edge; its trigger, input mask, blur copy (`AttachedSurface`), preview and fade would all move to or be shared with the new window. That is the "window reaching back to the screen edge needs its own design" the entry warns of, and it doubles each dock's windows to fix a look that shows only with a translucent bar on the dock's edge. Well past the rare-path cap of about 150 lines.

### Recommendation
A. The two surfaces already slide the way every detached box does on sway and niri, and the gap from Hyprland's popouts shows only through a translucent bar or border on the slide edge, which for the default toasts is usually no bar at all. B buys that look with a new layer rule, inset code and a mid-display layer jump; C adds a second window per dock. Under "Delete before adding" and "A rare path gets the smallest fix, or stays logged", the smallest fix is to stop calling it a gap. The measurement does turn up one real error worth the item: Hyprland re-reads `order` on an arrange, so the docs saying it reads it only at map should say what is true.

### Items
1. **Docs (trivial):** delete the issues.md entry and its roadmap link; held-off-edge.md › Sliding in from off the screen says the dock and detached toasts slide from their window's edge (the reserved line) on every compositor, and why (a reserving dock under the bars would push them in; toasts would jump layers while shown); correct "Hyprland reads `order` only as a surface maps" to "Hyprland applies a layer rule's `order` when it next arranges the layer; Quickshell sends the namespace only as a window maps, so a window whose namespace changes is remade" in `layer-arrangement.md`, `layering-and-corners.md`, `qml/components/bar/AGENTS.md`, `ClosedLatch.qml`'s comment and `scripts/gen_docs.py` (regenerating `generated/layers.md`).
