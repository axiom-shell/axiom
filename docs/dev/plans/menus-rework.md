# Menus rework

> **Issue:** [Menus rework](../issues.md#menus-rework)
> **Status:** decided: placement split, free menus, transparent menus, shortcuts, rename with aliases (2026-11-09); dragging and popping out edge menus (2026-11-10); review round 1 (2026-11-10)

### Problem
An edge menu's `mode` (`floating | integrated | submenu`, `qml/config/schema/definitions/layout/EdgeMenu.yaml`) answers two questions at once: where the menu lives, and what opens it. So:
- `submenu` really means "a menu with no home", but it is named after one of the things that opens it.
- A menu wanted only as a bar widget's popout still needs an edge, a screen, an offset and hover settings it never uses.
- `EdgeMenusConfig.popoutMenuOf` can never open a submenu menu.

The goal (maintainer, 2026-11-09) is one multipurpose **Menus** concept: a `placement` of `edge | strip | free`; a `free` menu with its own window, which can be pinned over windows and stays under the popouts opened over it; an optional transparent background, so the cards read as separate tiles; shortcuts that create a menu from the place that opens it; and the section renamed `EdgeMenus` → `Menus`.

### Options
#### A. Split `mode` into `placement`, give free menus a host (chosen)
`placement: edge | strip | free` says where a menu lives; what opens it (hover, a widget, a bind, a Submenu row) stays separate. A `free` menu gets its own window and placement helper, so a widget popout, a bind and a Submenu row can all open it. Costs a migration (v103) and a rename migration (v104); every read of `mode` changes.
#### B. Keep `mode`, add a fourth value for popout-only menus
No migration, but the mixed meaning stays and grows: each opener would need its own value, and `submenu` menus still couldn't be widget popouts.

### Recommendation
A, in six stages, one PR each. The maintainer's decisions (2026-11-09):
- "Rename with aliases: `edgeMenu` keeps working for IPC, the CLI and binds; it is the last stage."
- "Pinned edge menus: only `free` menus stay visible under popouts. Every edge menu, detached ones included, keeps today's rule: it hides when covered and comes back afterwards."

On dragging and popping out (2026-11-10, item 3b):
- "closed = docked makes sense to me. If you want extended floating make a floating menu. More of a one time thing. Should be a toggleable popout button that sits next to pin."
- On the close button: it docks the menu.
- Strip menus: "no strip popout allowed".
- Free-menu positions: "floating menus survive and remember their position (although they should have a 'draggable' toggle in their settings), only popped out floating edge menus don't survive reload and go back to their start when closed. normal floating menus return to their position essentially only on logout."
- The drag gaps take the recommended defaults, and "draggable needs to be conditionally visible in the settings UI based on if popout is enabled".

### Items
1. **`mode` → `placement`, behaviour unchanged.** `EdgeMenu.yaml`'s `mode` becomes `placement` (`edge | strip | free`, default `edge`), its `x-showIf` rules follow; migration v103 maps `floating→edge`, `integrated→strip`, `submenu→free`. `isSubmenu` → `EdgeMenusConfig.isFree`; a `free` menu behaves exactly as `submenu` did (no host, opened only by Submenu rows). The popout claim kind `"submenu"` (`PopoutGeometry`, `SubPopout`, `PopoutClaim`) is unrelated and stays.
2. **The free-menu window.** `surfaces/edgemenu/FreeMenu.qml` (a `PopoutWrapperBase` in its own full-screen `PanelWindow`, masked to its box, layer Top, namespace `axiom-menu`, `LayerSlot` held, keyboard on demand, `FocusGrab` only unpinned with `close.onOutsideClick`, hosting a `SubmenuHost`); a pure `methods/FreeMenuPlace.qml` with its test (the opener's rect and bar edge, `freeAnchor`, the box, screen and reserved zones in; the clamped origin and slide direction out); schema `freeAnchor` (`centre | cursor | point`, x/y %) and `freeAnimation` (`fade | scale | slide`); `popoutMenuOf` opens free menus at the widget; binds and IPC at `freeAnchor`; a `PopoutClaim` keyed `"menu:"+id`; `EdgeMenuSync` with an optional trigger; a `LayerStack` entry with its tests, goldens and blur lists; a new free-menus page in the edge menus docs.
3. **Pinned free menus stay under popouts** (dropped in review round 1, item 7). `PopoutGeometry.resolve`: a pinned free claim (`underlay`) never collides, with `tst_PopoutGeometry` rows; popouts draw above `axiom-menu`; a click on the menu closes an open popout and then reaches it; `EdgeMenuManager.canPin` allows free hosts. Dragging a pinned free menu is being specified again and moves to a later stage (2026-11-10).
3b. **Dragging, and popping out edge menus** (its underlay and close button changed in review round 1, item 7). `draggable` (default on; shown for `free`, and for `edge` with `popOutButton`, never `strip`): the box moves while dragged by its header (its top padding), pinned or not, nothing previewed. A free menu opened on its own keeps the dropped spot for the session (`EdgeMenuManager.positions`, `$XDG_RUNTIME_DIR` through `StateManager.createSessionHandler`, never config): binds, IPC and a pin reopening open it there; a widget's open stays at the widget and its drag isn't kept; the spot is clamped (`FreeMenuPlace`), dropped off its screen or once `freeAnchor` changes, and reset by the header's right-click Reset position or IPC `edgeMenu resetPosition <id>`. `popOutButton` (default off, `edge` only): a toggle beside the pin moves the open menu into the free host at its box, with no animation, the edge host kept until the free one shows; out, it's held as if pinned (an underlay) with a close, and every close docks it, closed; `open` and edge hover do nothing meanwhile; runtime only (`PersistentProperties`, a reload docks it closed); a fullscreen window only hides it. Pure decisions in `FreeMenuPlace` (`spot`, `keepsDrag`, `canDrag`, `underlay`, `hosts`) with `tst_FreeMenuPlace` rows, and a `tst_PopoutGeometry` row for the popped-out underlay.
4. **Transparent background.** `background: panel | none`, for `free` and `edge` (`none` forces detached, through `EdgeMenusConfig.detachedOf`), never `strip`; with `none` the host's surface is hollow (no fill, stroke or shadow) and each card sits on a tile of its own (`MenuTile`: the menu's `backgroundColor` at the card's shape with the box's shadow, a `BlurShape` rect when the blur window backs it, which on niri is a blur region piece per card), only with `moduleBorders` on (a bare module has no card); its input is still the whole box, the gaps between cards included (review round 1 dropped the first build's input mask on the cards alone, below). Pure decisions in `MenuTiles` (`tiled`, `detached`, `shown`) with `tst_MenuTiles`.
5. **Creation shortcuts** (the `key` purpose became `free` in review round 1, item 7). `EdgeMenuEditor.addMenu(purpose)` seeds `edge` (`open.onHover`), `popout`, `submenu` (free) or `key` (free, `freeAnchor.at: centre`) menus through the pure `MenuSeeds.seed` (`tst_MenuSeeds`); Layouts' "New menu" offers the four in a `StyledContextMenu`; a `key` menu is saved at once (`addSavedMenu`: a keybind may only open a saved menu), then `addKeybind` records its key. The Submenu module's `menu` field gets a "New submenu…" link (`addSubmenu`: a free menu in the same draft, set as the module's menu, then selected). The bar editor's edge menu pickers (a widget's `popout.menu`, a Button's click `menu`) get a "New menu…" link through `OverlayManager.newMenuFor(zone, index, path)`: the new menu saved at once (the saved config plus that item, `ConfigManager.commit`; the menu draft takes it as saved, `ConfigDraft.adopt`), the field set in the bar draft (`BarManager.updateWidgetProperty`, undoable), then Layouts on that menu. The links are `SettingsLink`s with a `run`, drawn under a field by a form's `fieldAction` hook (`SchemaPropertiesForm`, `OptionsGroup`). The pickers still list every menu.
6. **Rename `EdgeMenus` → `Menus`, with aliases.** Migration v104 (the section, keybind and Button `action: edgeMenu` → `menu`); `sections/Menus.yaml`; `EdgeMenusConfig` → `MenusConfig`, `EdgeMenuManager` → `MenuManager`, the host kind `edgeMenu` → `menu`, `surfaces/edgemenu` → `surfaces/menu`; IPC `menu` with `edgeMenu` forwarding, CLI `axiom menu` with `edge-menu` kept, the action table accepting `edgeMenu`; `ui/edgemenus.json` → `ui/menus.json`, read from the old name once; the edge menus docs to a menus folder.
7. **Review round 1** (maintainer, 2026-11-10). Quoted:
   - "Bars must not move/redraw when making an action. They will respect blocking other menus to open according to the priority (i.e. launcher can probably close it)"; a pinned or popped-out free menu under a bar widget's popout: "Menu hides, comes back". No underlay: a free menu ranks as any popout (unpinned as its opener's kind; pinned or popped out as a pinned menu, `FreeMenuPlace.ranksPinned`, engaged as `EdgePopout`'s); the claim's underlay flag, its check in `PopoutGeometry` and the overlay's exemption for it went. `tst_PopoutGeometry`'s `test_free_menu`.
   - "opened by a keybind option needs to be replaced by free when adding new menus": New menu's `key` purpose became `free` (`freeAnchor.at: centre`, no keybind, unsaved).
   - "It should just reserve the mouse (i.e. the ability to click through / focus through can be removed)": a tiled menu's input is its whole box; `CardMask`, `EdgePopout.maskRegions`, the close delay's floor and `MenuTiles`' aim and union went.
   - "the background windows should still be usable and focusable while a free menu is open"; with close-on-outside-click on, a click on a window: "Close + reach window". The free menu's `FocusGrab` (Hyprland's grab, the backdrop catching clicks elsewhere, Exclusive keyboard) was the desync with a held, popped-out menu, which never took it. It closes through `FocusLeave` instead: the compositor focusing a window, or the keyboard leaving it after it held it.
   - "the open delay can use the default value configured in popouts and removed as a setting": `open.delay` went; config v103 (unshipped) drops it.
   - "when a menu is popped out, having to buttons is counter intuitive - it can just keep the dock button (close is unecessary). It should also remain in the same location when clicked": what stays put is "The button" (the buttons keep the edge's corner while popped out).
   - "free menu should have option for a close button": `closeButton` (free menus only), last in the row, closing it unpinned.
